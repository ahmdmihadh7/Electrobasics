Why is Power Factor Crucial in AC Grids?

Power factor represents the ratio of real power (doing actual work) to apparent power (total power supplied). A poor power factor draws higher current for the same work, increasing I²R transmission losses and requiring costly capacitor correction.

Power Factor (cos φ) = Real Power (P) / Apparent Power (S)
